Help & Assistance With Your Fees

Get In Touch

We Offer great Advice On Care Fees In Dartford, Bexley and Greenwich.

When it comes to funding the care of your loved one, it can be daunting to know where to begin and where to get the right information. At Refined Care Services we support you and advise you on the possible options. There are several options available to fund your care.

Social care Funding

To work out what will enable you to stay in the comfort of your home longer the local council can assess you and if they agree that you are eligible for home care support they will pay towards your social care depending on your savings. For more information, you call the Disability Rights UK Personal Budgets Helpline (0330 995 0404).

Get In Touch

Private funding

You can arrange and pay for the care service yourself if you do not qualify to receive government funding. We are here to help you understand the cost and plan effectively.

Get In Touch

NHS continuing health care funding

This is for adults Individuals with long-term complex health needs are eligible for free social care arranged and funded solely by the Clinical Commissioning Group (CCG).

You are eligible for NHS continuing healthcare depending on your assessed needs and not based on your condition.

For more information visit Beacon website or phone the NHS helpline on 0345 5480300.
