Are you passionate about making a difference in people's lives? Do you have a caring nature and a desire to support others? If so, we have an exciting opportunity for you! About Us

At Refined Care Services, we are committed to delivering high-quality care and support services to individuals across the UK. We believe in empowering our clients to live fulfilling lives and strive to make a positive impact in their daily lives. As part of our team, you will have the opportunity to work with a diverse range of clients and make a real difference in their lives.

Position Overview

As a carer, your primary responsibilities will include assisting clients with personal care tasks, such as bathing, dressing, and toileting, as well as providing companionship and emotional support. You will also be responsible for helping clients with household tasks, medication management, and accompanying them to appointments or outings as needed.
