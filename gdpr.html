GDPR Statement

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Statement

We are proud to provide high quality care to our service users.

Data protection regulation has been in force in the United Kingdom since May 2018. This legislation ensures that personal data from service users are collected, processed and stored responsibly.

Refined Care Services LTD takes privacy seriously and will only use personal information for what it is consented to.

We will never share or use any personal data other than those described here. We go to great lengths to ensure that personal information is used in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We will always obtain consent to access, process, store or share any personal data.

We have a policy and procedure to process personal data, ensuring that personal data is not breached.

Personal Data Summary

What personal data will we collect?

We will collect the following data:

  • Your name
  • Addresses
  • Date of birth
  • Payment details (including card or bank information for transfers and direct debits)
  • Health information (including dietary requirements, allergies and health conditions)
  • Photographs or video recordings
  • Identification documents

For employees

  • Contact details (eg name, address, telephone number or personal email address)
  • Date of birth
  • National Insurance number
  • Copies of passports or other photo ID
  • employment history (eg job application, employment references or secondary employment)
  • Education history (eg qualifications)
  • Right to work information
  • Details of any criminal convictions (eg Disclosure Barring Service (DBS), Access NI or Disclosure Scotland checks)
  • Security clearance details (eg basic checks and higher security clearance).
Who will use my data?

Refined Care Services LTD
What will the data be used for?

Your data will be stored and used to create a personalised care plan for you, in order to provide care for you.

We process employee's data to meet a legal obligation and fulfil obligations under the contract.
What data will be shared?

We will not share your data with a third party. We will only share your data with other health professionals, care agencies, regulatory bodies or legal bodies according to need.  This will always be done with your consent or the person that is legally authorised to make decisions on your behalf

How long will my data be kept?

Your data will be stored in accordance with the data retention and erasure policy.
Who can access my data?

Your data will only be accessed by authorised people/organisations.
How will my data be kept responsibly?

Your data will be stored under a secured server with a password even when shared with other healthcare organisations or legal authorities.

Data Protection Principles

  1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
  2. Purpose limitation
  3. Data minimisation
  4. Accuracy and up to date
  5. Storage limitation kept only as long as needed
  6. Integrity and confidentiality
  7. Accountability kept in a responsible manner

Information We hold about you

We may collect and store information on you in order to provide care for the service users or to meet employment contract obligations. The information we collect, process and store are described in the data protection summary.

Your data protection rights

Under data protection law, you have rights including:

Your right of access - You have the right to ask us for copies of your personal data.

Your right to rectification - You have the right to ask us to rectify personal data you think is inaccurate. You also have the right to ask us to complete information you think is incomplete.

Your right to erasure - You have the right to ask us to erase your personal data in certain circumstances.

Your right to restriction of processing - You have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

Your right to object to processing - You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

Your right to data portability - You have the right to ask that we transfer the personal data you gave us to another organisation, or to you, in certain circumstances.

Your right to withdraw consent – When we use consent as our lawful basis you have the right to withdraw your consent.

You don’t usually need to pay a fee to exercise your rights. If you make a request, we have one calendar month to respond to you.

To make a data protection rights request, please contact us on 013 2287 9465 or email us at info@refinedcareservices.co.uk.

How to complain

If you have any concerns about our use of your personal data, you can make a complaint to us using the contact provided here Tel: 013 2287 9465 or email us at: info@refinedcareservices.co.uk.
