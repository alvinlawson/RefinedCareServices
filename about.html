Our story

It all started with the passion for making a difference and a simple question: How can we help better the lives of the elderly and those with health-related issues maintain their independence by providing a better home care service?

This desire to make a positive change was the beginning of the company called Refined Care Services. With that in mind we set out our core values, our mission statement and our values as a guide for us and our staff and also a point of reference for our clients.

Our Vision and Mission

Our mission is to promote independence and the quality of life, one individual at a time.

We believe that encouraging our clients to be self-reliant by encouraging them to do for themselves as much as they are capable, in their daily living activities, will boost confidence in themselves and increase self-esteem.

We are very keen on promoting independence. Our teams will ensure that you receive outstanding care tailored to your needs and preferences, providing structure and reassurance through compassion, kindness and respect whilst remaining flexible in the changing circumstances that may occur.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Do the right thing at all times
  • Commitment: Dedicated to care and serving no matter the circumstances
  • Respect: Building trusted relationships through open communication and respecting other's views
  • Inclusion: Given equal opportunity and access to all regardless of race, sexuality, disability and gender
  • Collaboration: Working in partnership to produce the best outcome in service
  • Innovation: Embracing the new and influencing changes

Refined Care Services supports both private clients and those supported by Social Services. Each are treated equally and to exceptional standards. We operate throughout Dartford, Bexley and Greenwich. If you would like to chat about care services please do not hesitate to contact us. We will tailor a care package that suits you perfectly.
