Exceptional And Flexible Home Care Services

Refined Care Services offers a broad range of professional care services throughout Greenwich, Bexley and Dartford.

Our commitment to care at the highest level our flexibility and custom approach to every client has seen an organic growth and a level of care that e are justifiably proud of. We offer specialised customised care in the following services for clients living at home.

Visiting Care

With visiting care, our professional carer will visit you at home at specific times to support your personal care and other daily living activities that you need assistance with. We provide long and short visits depending on your care needs.

Overnight Care

Darkness increases the fall risk for elderly people. It is during the night that some Senior people feel vulnerable and anxious, which can disturb a good night's sleep.

Our team at Refined Care Services provide care, especially for those who have additional needs overnight. The care and support can range from toileting, change of linen in case of an accident or blood glucose checks. You tell us what you need and we will provide it.

Get In Touch

Respite Care

Caring for a loved one day in and day out can be exhausting, sometimes you need that break for yourself to recharge.

We are there to help you and take over the care of your loved one as long as it may require, so you can take time out to look after yourself.
