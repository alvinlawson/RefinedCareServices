Need special care? We can help.

We are a professional, nursing led, care provider with specialist skills in many areas. Here are examples of the specialist type of work we do for our clients.

Cognitive, Alzheimer & Dementia Care

Dementia is an umbrella term for symptoms associated with a progressive decline of brain functions such as memory, thinking, behaviour, skills and mental abilities.

Caring for a family member with Alzheimer's, Huntington's disease and other cognitive impairment disorders can be extremely difficult. We understand that looking after a family member with personality change, suspicious behaviour, hallucinations, communication difficulties and confusion can feel like you are fighting a losing battle. It can be emotionally and physically draining.

We are here to provide your loved one the structure that they need to give them a sense of normalcy.

We provide customised care plans with a combination of personal care, music therapy, brain games therapy and companionship.

Adults with physical Disability

Physical disability can be hereditary through chromosome disorder, degenerative health conditions or acquired at some point in life.

Dealing with a physical disability can be extremely challenging. Performing specific daily living activities can take longer to complete, which can be frustrating.

Whether you have a traumatic brain injury, stroke or neuromuscular disorder, whatever the physical disability is, we are here to support you. The team at Refined Care Services has the right support, knowledge, understanding and skills to help you navigate your day-to-day activities.

Our professional team will come and assess your needs and with careful planning provide you with the care that is tailored to you.

End of Life Care at Home

End-of-life care is support for individuals who are approaching the last months or years of their life.

It focuses on improving quality of life through symptom management, assistance and providing comfort. Hearing that an advanced incurable illness does not respond to treatment or that your end days are nearing can be devastating.

Many people who require end-of-life care prefer to spend their remaining days in the familiar environment of their home. The decision to spend their last days at home can be psychologically, physically and emotionally challenging for loved ones.

Our end-of-life service will provide you and your loved ones, with care, support, and comfort until the end.

Reablement (Care after illness or hospital discharge)

Reablement care is intermediate care for individuals to rehabilitate, regain confidence, strength and learn new skills after a hospital stay.

Sometimes long-term hospital admission after an illness or surgery for the elderly can reduce mobility and functional decline. You may need temporary care and support to get back on your feet.

Recuperating at home after illness or surgery is made accessible with the support of our carers. We deliver short-term and flexible care options based on your needs.

Whether it is assistance with mobility, daily activities such as washing, dressing, cleaning or meal preparations.
We are here to support you, to prevent readmission and to save your energy for recovery.
